Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Spentup is built for Indian college students. We believe your financial data is personal. We collect the minimum data needed to make the app work — and nothing more.

What data we collect

Spentup stores all your data locally on your device using your browser's localStorage. This includes:

  • Your first name (entered during onboarding)
  • Your monthly budget amount
  • Expense entries you log (amount, category, note, date)
  • Recurring expenses you set up
  • App preferences (notification settings, selected categories)

This data never leaves your device unless you use the voice logging feature (see below).

Voice logging and AI

Spentup includes an optional voice expense logging feature. When you use it:

  • Your voice is converted to text by your device's built-in speech recognition (Google or Apple)
  • The text transcript is sent to our server, which forwards it to a third-party AI service (Groq) for parsing
  • The AI extracts the expense amount, category, and note from your spoken words
  • We do not store your voice recordings or transcripts after processing
  • Groq's privacy policy applies to data processed by their service: groq.com/privacy-policy

The voice feature is optional. You can use Spentup entirely without it.

Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your bank account or UPI details
  • We do not read your SMS or transaction messages
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not use analytics or advertising trackers
  • We do not sell your data to anyone
  • We do not create accounts — there is no Spentup server storing your data

Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, Spentup uses your browser's notification API to send local reminders at 9am and 9pm. These are scheduled on your device and do not involve any server communication.

Third party services

  • Groq — AI parsing for voice expense logging (optional feature only)
  • Google Fonts — fonts loaded from Google's CDN
  • Vercel — app hosting

Children's privacy

Spentup is intended for college students (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes to this privacy policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
